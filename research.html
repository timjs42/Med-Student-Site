Research Overview

As a medical student at Texas Christian University School of Medicine, my research focuses on innovative approaches to cardiovascular health, neurological disorders, and emerging infectious diseases. I am passionate about translational research that bridges the gap between laboratory discoveries and clinical applications.

"The goal of my research is not just to understand disease mechanisms, but to develop practical solutions that can improve patient outcomes and quality of life."

Below you'll find information about my current research projects, publications, and academic collaborations. I'm always open to discussing potential research partnerships and mentorship opportunities.

Current Projects

  • Novel Biomarkers for Early Detection of Cardiovascular Disease Principal Investigator | 2023 - Present

    Investigating plasma microRNA profiles as potential early indicators of atherosclerosis progression in high-risk populations.

  • Neural Correlates of Cognitive Decline in Aging Research Associate | 2022 - Present

    Using advanced neuroimaging techniques to identify predictive markers of cognitive impairment before clinical symptoms appear.

  • Immunological Response to SARS-CoV-2 Variants Co-Investigator | 2021 - Present

    Analyzing T-cell responses to emerging viral variants to better understand long-term immunity and vaccine efficacy.
