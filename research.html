Research Overview

As a medical student at TCU's Burnett School of Medicine and a former paramedic, my research centers on opioid overdose response and naloxone access, alongside a broad range of clinical and surgical outcomes research with collaborators across several institutions.

"I'm absolutely sure that we're saving more lives than are reported" — on why better data on naloxone use matters.

Below you'll find my current thesis project, publications, and a selection of the conference presentations and honors from four years of research.

Current Projects

  • The Impact of Naloxone Trainings on Opioid Overdoses Scholarly Pursuit & Thesis | June 2023 – May 2027

    Four-year thesis project studying how naloxone training affects response effectiveness, developed with Drew's 27 Chains and faculty mentor Dr. Ric Bonnell — including a QR-code system on naloxone kits to track outcomes.

  • Health-Related Quality of Life Frameworks Research Collaboration | 2025

    A series of theoretical papers applying frameworks like Maslow's hierarchy and Bronfenbrenner's ecological systems theory to chronic illness and pediatric quality of life, presented at the ISOQOL 32nd Annual Conference.
