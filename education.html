Research Project 2023-2024

Medication Adherence in Geriatric Patients

Quality Improvement 2023

Conducted a quality improvement project to enhance medication adherence among geriatric patients with multiple chronic conditions. Designed simplified medication schedules and visual aids for patients with cognitive impairments.

Implementation led to 28% improvement in adherence rates over 6 months
