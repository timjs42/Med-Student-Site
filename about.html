My Journey

I'm Angel Sheu, a dedicated medical student at TCU School of Medicine with a passion for patient care, clinical research, and healthcare accessibility. My journey into medicine began during my undergraduate studies at the University of Texas, where I developed a deep interest in human biology and the complexities of healthcare delivery.

Growing up in a family with limited access to healthcare resources, I witnessed firsthand the challenges that many communities face when seeking medical attention. These early experiences shaped my perspective and inspired my commitment to becoming a physician who prioritizes both medical excellence and compassionate care.

"My goal is to combine rigorous clinical practice with innovative research to improve healthcare outcomes for underserved populations."
