My Clinical Journey

My journey in medicine has been shaped by diverse clinical experiences that have strengthened my commitment to patient care and medical excellence. Each opportunity has provided unique insights into different aspects of healthcare delivery and reinforced my passion for becoming a compassionate physician.

Six years in the field as an EMT and paramedic before ever setting foot in a medical school classroom — treating patients first, then learning the science behind it.
