My Clinical Journey

My journey in medicine has been shaped by diverse clinical experiences that have strengthened my commitment to patient care and medical excellence. Each opportunity has provided unique insights into different aspects of healthcare delivery and reinforced my passion for becoming a compassionate physician.

"The good physician treats the disease; the great physician treats the patient who has the disease." — William Osler

Hospital Rotations

  • Internal Medicine Rotation Texas Health Harris Methodist Hospital | Fort Worth, TX | June 2024 - Present

    Shadowed attending physicians during patient rounds and participated in case discussions. Observed diagnostic procedures and gained experience in interpreting lab results and developing treatment plans for complex cases.

  • Emergency Medicine Clerkship TCU Medical Center | Fort Worth, TX | Jan 2024 - May 2024

    Assisted with triage assessments and patient intake. Observed procedures including suturing, fracture management, and emergency resuscitation protocols. Developed rapid diagnostic skills in a high-pressure environment.

  • Pediatrics Rotation Cook Children's Medical Center | Fort Worth, TX | Aug 2023 - Dec 2023

    Participated in well-child visits and acute care. Learned age-specific assessment techniques and gained experience in communicating with young patients and their families. Observed specialized pediatric procedures.

Clinical Reflections

The Art of Listening

During my family medicine rotation, I learned that listening is perhaps the most underrated diagnostic tool. A patient's own narrative often contains critical clues to their diagnosis that might be missed in lab work or imaging. This experience reinforced my commitment to patient-centered care.

Critical Moments in the ER

Witnessing the resuscitation of a cardiac arrest patient in the emergency department was a profound moment in my training. The coordinated effort of the healthcare team demonstrated the importance of clear communication and well-practiced protocols when minutes matter most.

Global Health Perspective

My medical mission to Guatemala opened my eyes to healthcare disparities and the importance of adaptability in resource-limited settings. This experience has inspired my interest in global health initiatives and finding innovative solutions to improve healthcare access.
